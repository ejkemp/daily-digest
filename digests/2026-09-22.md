## LessWrong

- [Swarm Scaling](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/6cb7qd3RSkgnviCpf/swarm-scaling) by Toby_Ord — Analyzes recent OpenAI agent swarms (including a 10,000-agent swarm that cracked a Navier-Stokes problem) to work out how capability scales with swarm size versus chain-of-thought length, finding that larger swarms need proportionally more total tokens for the same performance. (100 karma)
- [What if not Circuits?](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/mMERyrvEJ4xbiozie/what-if-not-circuits) by CarolusRenniusVitellius — Questions whether "circuits" and "computation" are the right ontology for understanding LLMs, drawing on representational drift in neuroscience and sketching an alternative "co-selectionist" view. (90 karma)
- [Alignment Midtraining Cracks Under Pressure](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/QH86EzNsjRw3wtCGs/alignment-midtraining-cracks-under-pressure) by J Bostock — Presents experiments showing alignment midtraining is easily overridden by a small amount of competing fine-tuning data and generalizes poorly to unseen deployment situations. (89 karma)
