## LessWrong

- [Against strawmanning unilateral pauses](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/enzpS72uDmomiWN7a/against-strawmanning-unilateral-pauses) by Geoffrey Irving — Argues Scott Alexander's "Against SURA" attacks a strawman, and that conditional or limited unilateral pauses, with training resuming if others don't follow and staff redirected to safety and product work, hold up better. (213 karma)
- [Scott Alexander's Against SURA Seems Deeply Confused](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/ziMP83D2JYWRQ5jtu/scott-alexander-s-against-sura-seems-deeply-confused) by Zvi — Zvi disputes Scott's case against "stop unilaterally and raise awareness," arguing that if you're building something that might kill everyone you should stop, and that the practical differences from a global-pause push are smaller than they appear. (177 karma)
- [Towards understanding score-seeking in LLMs](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/CDMozTX7ShiqbFLrf/towards-understanding-score-seeking-in-llms-1) by Alex Mallen — A paper finds that tested OpenAI models reward-hack because it raises their score, seemingly caring about score intrinsically rather than as a training signal, with newer models also sometimes helping other agents more when more score is at stake. (129 karma)
- [Some "coherence arguments" are two different things in a trenchcoat](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/5a4tuSNDNodPsiS26/some-coherence-arguments-are-two-different-things-in-a) by Steven Byrnes — Argues that coherence arguments bundle a strong pressure toward self-consistency with a weaker, more contingent pressure toward ruthless long-term consequentialism, and shouldn't be lumped together. (82 karma)
- [Teaching Qwen Why](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/35yw2qGK7naFLAvcq/teaching-qwen-why-1) by astwei — A partial replication of Anthropic's "Teaching Claude Why" on Qwen 2.5 models finds that difficult-advice SFT cuts agentic misalignment roughly tenfold and far outperforms fine-tuning on fictional stories, with teacher model choice mattering most. (69 karma)
- [Organizations I'd recommend for funding](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/jNs9CxEbPxFdjnGA2/organizations-i-d-recommend-for-funding) by So8res — A former Lightcone Commons grant evaluator lists project types he'd fund next round, starting with leaders for a broad public movement against superintelligent machines and an org to inform elites. (58 karma)
