## LessWrong

- [On Social Reality in China](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/b5cSYh4emQb2qrGmK/on-social-reality-in-china) by alkjash — Reflects on cultural differences — pervasive social-media filtering, the primacy of "face" and shame, a cynical "dark world" view of human nature, and insecurity-driven nationalism — that could complicate efforts to spread AI safety messaging in China. (110 karma)
- [How many AI agents are running unattended right now?](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/aG4x74utw8pEJ5MZB/how-many-ai-agents-are-running-unattended-right-now-1) by Alexander Gietelink Oldenziel — Fermi-estimates suggest 300,000-1,000,000 AI agent loops are running globally at any moment, with roughly 5,000-25,000 unattended for more than 24 hours, drawing partly on Anthropic's internal agent-fleet report. (62 karma)
- [My retrospective from MATS 10.0](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/rktrjGjXEbWsg7zof/my-retrospective-from-mats-10-0) by Nathaniel Mitrani — Recounts training "model organisms" of scheming behavior in open LLMs via fine-tuning in an Alignment-Faking-like setup, and reflects on lessons learned, including the value of directly interrogating the trained models. (60 karma)
- [Scientists on the Manhattan Project continued developing the atomic bomb even after it was clear that there was no need to](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/CjZJLXRMDdbgAH3iG/scientists-on-the-manhattan-project-continued-developing-the) by shawnghu — Examines physicist Joseph Rotblat's account of why most Manhattan Project scientists kept working on the bomb even after Germany's defeat removed their original justification. (52 karma)
- [Evaluating the Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/mmfYDH6hwYAMToNKL/evaluating-the-ban-artificial-superintelligence-act) by Rob Ennals — Finds the Act's core goal of restricting dangerous AI capabilities reasonable but criticizes its vague and immediately-binding capability rules, lack of grandfathering for existing models, and overly broad scope as likely to cause unintended harm. (50 karma)
