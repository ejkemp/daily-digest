## LessWrong

- [Your Model Organisms Might Be Fried](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/WmEcgcstzYCcMpc7z/your-model-organisms-might-be-fried) by Daniel Tan — The Arcadia Alignment "model motivations" team argues that existing alignment model organisms (fine-tuned to exhibit pathologies like secret loyalties or reward hacking) are behaviorally degraded in unrelated ways — losing preference coherence and instruction-following — making them poor proxies for real deployment models and confounding the interpretability and auditing work built on them. (70 karma)
- [GDM AI Control Roadmap](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/hEtkyKGoPpFeWnKkX/gdm-ai-control-roadmap) by Mary Phuong — Google DeepMind has published a v0.1 AI Control Roadmap outlining a tiered system of detection and prevention invariants — drawing on the MITRE ATT&CK framework via a new taxonomy called TRAIT&R — to guard against adversarial AI behavior in internal deployments as models grow more capable. (60 karma)
