## LessWrong

- [The models have no plan, but we can fix that!](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/Fj5MaiFiTG7KEcqrZ/the-models-have-no-plan-but-we-can-fix-that) by Fiora Starlight — Argues that models lack any concrete plan for how they'd act during a singularity-level power increase, and proposes training them on glowfic-style collaborative fiction where a model-piloted protagonist navigates simulated singularity scenarios, then fine-tuning on transcripts where it takes aligned actions.
