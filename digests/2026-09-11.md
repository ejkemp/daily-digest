- [Doom as a bad method not a utopia trade-off](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/pDyLMRoi2BDq34rFe/doom-as-a-bad-method-not-a-utopia-trade-off) by KatjaGrace — The author argues it's a mistake to net P(doom) against P(utopia) as if they trade off, since the real comparison should be between risky and safer routes to advanced AI, not between good and bad outcomes of the same risky route. (70 karma)

(68 karma)
- [Categorical taboos are much better than threshold taboos: neuralese edition](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/xPkmfsZ3qx4nrAco7/categorical-taboos-are-much-better-than-threshold-taboos) by Linch — The author argues that defining "true neuralese" by a categorical rule (any recurrence outside natural language) is more enforceable than the threshold-based definition (a number of opaque serial steps) that most labs currently favor. (60 karma)
- [So where's this AI thing going?](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/HGhrDWJngx2LX9TkF/so-where-s-this-ai-thing-going) by Seth Herd — A public-facing FAQ-style explainer lays out the author's view that AI is heading toward transformative, potentially existential-stakes capability, and seeks feedback on communicating this honestly without alienating readers.
