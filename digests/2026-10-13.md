## LessWrong

- [OpenAI Can't Self-Police Because It Fires the People Raising the Alarm](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/sSHbioPoHWtm4zuti/openai-can-t-self-police-because-it-fires-the-people-raising) by TurnTrout — A Fortune op-ed arguing that OpenAI's firing of three safety researchers shortly after endorsing independent evaluation shows voluntary self-policing doesn't work. (201 karma)
- [Labs' current alignment plans seem terrible](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/xyg3ecpFNuGr5q8Zq/labs-current-alignment-plans-seem-terrible) by Daniel Tan — Argues that frontier labs' implicit plan of character shaping, heavy RL, and racing toward prosaic recursive self-improvement is deeply flawed, and that evidence on how it will go is worth collecting. (134 karma)
- [Research Lessons I Learned at MATS & Redwood](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/Q4rcvgjbgjTwmn8Xa/research-lessons-i-learned-at-mats-and-redwood) by Jo Jiao — Argues empirical safety research is bottlenecked on clear thinking, and that building predictive, falsifiable models and updating them with uncertainty in mind speeds up iteration. (129 karma)
- [Quantity vs quality in pottery is a lie](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/LrAqM4bGmAoA79rP3/quantity-vs-quality-in-pottery-is-a-lie) by becausecurious — Traces the famous pottery anecdote from *Art & Fear* to show it was never a study and was originally a photography-class story that the coauthors changed to ceramics. (110 karma)
- [More people should write basic AI safety explainers](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/yLdgq9uAxDHzqXdjj/more-people-should-write-basic-ai-safety-explainers) by Linch — Argues the community underrates writing clear, simple explainers of core AI safety ideas, since different explanations convince different people and good ones are still scarce. (83 karma)
- [Base Models Can Detect Hidden Intentions that Prompted Classifiers Miss](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/YvNkA3ZzsCv9QQmXR/base-models-can-detect-hidden-intentions-that-prompted) by Oz Amram — Shows that scoring responses under a base model with a persona header can decode subliminal signals (e.g., owl vs. eagle preference) that prompted classifiers miss, with AUCs of 0.84–0.94. (70 karma)
