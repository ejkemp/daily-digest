([discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50121179), 508 points)
- [How the Claude Code team does code review](https://claude.dev/blog/solving-code-review-at-anthropic/) — The team describes moving most code reading to automated review and verification, aiming for at most one human in the loop per PR, while merged PRs more than doubled from June to September and the revert share fell by over 40 percent.
