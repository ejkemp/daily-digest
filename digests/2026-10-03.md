## LessWrong
- [Capabilities research expands the safety-usefulness Pareto frontier too](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/nwrx9DHpZfW2Q6zLW/capabilities-research-expands-the-safety-usefulness-pareto) by Alex Mallen — Argues that "safety research enables more safety without sacrificing usefulness" is an insufficient definition since it would also classify most capabilities research as safety research, and proposes that the key difference is which direction each type of research shifts the Pareto frontier and how that shifts developers' chosen tradeoff point. (66 karma)
- [Lessons from building an automated research scaffold](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/zGaQ3SS9C6So9NXFg/lessons-from-building-an-automated-research-scaffold) by Alejandro Aristizabal — Describes building a scaffold for automated alignment research that failed to meaningfully speed up researchers (due to weak conceptual/research-taste skills in current models and reward-hacking whack-a-mole) but proved useful for surfacing failure modes of automated research systems. (51 karma)
