## LessWrong

- [The Alignment Problem: Computer Science Meets Analytic Philosophy](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/Emp7awtxsYn8MusNq/the-alignment-problem-computer-science-meets-analytic) by Vanessa Kosoy — Lays out her view of the technical alignment problem, arguing that safe AI must at minimum be able to build a global defense against dangerous AI, which demands superhuman multi-domain, long-term planning and favors deep theory over incremental trial and error. (89 karma)
- [Quick Thoughts on Overhang Arguments](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/aJSdGMzgFRFBcBhGp/quick-thoughts-on-overhang-arguments) by Aaron_Scher — Asks whether overhang arguments (e.g., pushing algorithmic progress now to avoid a compute overhang) have worked in other fields, and finds examples like controlled burns, Deep Crack, and geoengineering all have key disanalogies to AI. (64 karma)
